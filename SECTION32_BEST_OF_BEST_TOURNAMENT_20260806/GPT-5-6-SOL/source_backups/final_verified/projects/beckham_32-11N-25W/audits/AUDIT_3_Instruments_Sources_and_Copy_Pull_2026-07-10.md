The **NHE candidate is the better working draft**, because its runsheet is clearer, its evidence-tier labels are materially better, and its exceptions are more explicit. - The base candidate duplicates Bk 2476/P1. NHE properly removes the duplicate.
- Both candidates omit the Bk 1016/P7, P13, P18, P25, P29, and P33 bridge instruments, most modern mortgages/UCCs/releases, all four unresolved countywide long-description/no-legal records, the complete fee/mineral chain, operative schedules, lease terms, payout facts, and current lien/continuation evidence.

## Evidence hierarchy and notation

- **D — direct recorded image:** the operative local image was reviewed. Highest available local proof of text, subject to legibility and complete-page-range confirmation.
- **M — index/public metadata:** proves a facial county record identifier, recording date, indexed parties/type/page range, and coverage clues. It does **not** prove the operative grant, schedule, estate, fraction, exclusions, reservations, or title effect.
- **O — OCR:** finding aid only. OCR errors are common and OCR is never elevated over the image.
- **A — assumption:** expressly labeled workflow hypothesis only; not a recorded fact or title conclusion.

## What may and may not be assumed

### Permissible, expressly labeled working assumptions

1. **Applicability pending schedule:** if the section index marks Section 32 or public metadata says long description/no legal, carry the record as potentially applicable until its schedule rules it out.
2. **No quantitative inference:** use `OPEN`/blank for fraction, WI, NRI, net acres, depth, and current owner unless the operative instrument proves it.
3. **Conservative burden treatment:** assume an identified ORRI, reversion, payout interest, mortgage, or UCC remains potentially effective until a matched release/termination and successor chain proves otherwise.
4. **Separate branches:** treat Leede/Exxon/NICOR/St. Mary/Kabala, Chesapeake/FourPoint/Unbridled/MNR, Tapstone/KL CHK/Diversified, Burlington/DP Legacy Central, and Canvas/DP Ponies as parallel branches until schedules show overlap or succession.
5. **Agreement/affidavit/memorandum limitation:** treat these as notice/evidence, not title conveyances, absent operative granting or merger language.

### Assumptions that are not defensible

- “All 16 quarter-quarter ticks” equals 100% WI, all depths, all wells, all leases, fee minerals, or present ownership.
- An active OCC well proves any particular lease is HBP.
- A public merger/affidavit row automatically vests title in the named public/surviving entity.
- A financing termination releases every county mortgage/UCC or every item in a blanket collateral package.
- Bk 1014/P75 conveyed a WI merely because it purports to do so; the assignor's source title must be proven.
- A missing section-index entry excludes a countywide long-description or no-legal-description record.
- Assessor surface ownership establishes mineral ownership.

## Source-quality caveats

- `Blank Index.pdf` and `Protocoled Index.pdf` have different containers/sizes but page-for-page identical visible images. They are not independent corroborating sources.
- The purported Tax Roll is a duplicate of the assessor parcel map and supplies no additional tax-roll evidence.
- OCR is useful for locating text, but examples in this matter show obvious recognition errors. Every date, name, fraction, depth, book/page, and operative clause must be checked against the image.
- Public county metadata is strong for record identity and range, but it is not an abstract of the operative instrument. Watermarked viewer availability does not mean a complete copy/schedule was reviewed.
- The report should use confidence by proposition (identity, text, quantum, current effect), not one percentage for an entire instrument.
