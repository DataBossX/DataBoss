## 6. Assumptions permitted in the rebuilt report

Only the following limited assumptions are made, and each is labeled:

- NE/4 = 160 nominal acres, E/2 SE/4 = 80 nominal acres, N/2 = 320 nominal acres, and the section = 640 nominal acres for geographic organization only.
- Nominal aliquot acreage is not net mineral acreage, leasehold acreage, or ownership.
- An index hit or metadata record shows a potential facial connection only; it does not prove that the subject, a particular depth, wellbore, fraction, or estate is in an attached schedule.
- Entity-name similarity is a search lead, not proof of merger, succession, or legal identity.
- A release or termination is not applied to an asset unless its operative collateral schedule matches the asset.
- Regulatory operator/well data are not used as title, allocation, or HBP proof.
- Blank ownership fields mean **not determinable from supplied evidence**, never zero.

No other assumptions should be made without new record evidence and a written assumption note identifying its purpose, basis, affected cells, and risk.
