# Section 32 Images 0001–0500 Assumptions and Gaps

This report lists disclosed extraction assumptions and unresolved issues. It must be read with the final workbook. Blank fields were not invented, and no fractions were manufactured to force an ownership balance.

## Major gaps

- **No Diversified succession bridge**: No assignment, merger, name-change filing, acquisition schedule, probate, conveyance, or operator record links Diversified to The Carter Oil Company, its seven assignees, Consolidated Gas Utilities Corporation, any mineral grantee, or any ORRI owner.
- **No current lease-status or production evidence**: The extracts do not establish whether the historical leases expired, were held by production, pooled, communitized, extended, released, or superseded; no current division order, pay deck, JIB, operating agreement, spacing/pooling order, well file, or production record is included.
- **Missing pages and fragments**: Multiple early instruments are opening-only, ending-only, acknowledgment-only, faint, or lack adjacent recorded pages, including B04-G002 (0170), B05-G002/B05-G005/B05-G010/B05-G014 (0210/0214/0217/0221), B07-G003/B07-G005 (0338/0340), and several Batch 01-03 low-confidence fragments. Their absent parties, legal descriptions, and effects were not inferred.
- **Continuation beyond image 0500**: B10-G032, the Seventh Supplemental Indenture, is only pages 225-236 in images 0494-0500 and ends mid-sentence. Property schedules, Section 32 applicability, execution, acknowledgment, and ending are outside scope.
- **Exhibits and referenced source instruments**: Many leases, prior deeds, mortgages, supplements, operating agreements, and schedules are referenced but not reproduced. The September 1, 1950 operating agreement referenced by B10-G019 is particularly material to WI/NRI and burdens.
- **Conflicting or unresolved record facts**: B05-G007 and B05-G009 conflict over whether the April 1936 SE/4 release was recorded; B07-G002 contains Washita/Beckham county language conflict; B08-G039 states an acreage inconsistent with its fraction; B10-G026 lists layered mineral exclusions that may exhaust the described 160 mineral acres; B10-G005's 54/640 reading is scan-soft.
- **Illegibility and uncertain indexing**: Faint handwriting, negative images, compressed schedules, unresolved aliquots, missing recording data, and unidentified parties affect several records. B10-G029 (0491) lacks its complete mortgage, recording data, acknowledgment, and release.
- **No depth, formation, or wellbore chain**: The reviewed instruments do not establish any present depth-limited, formation-limited, or wellbore-only interest, nor any well-specific WI/NRI.
- **No complete ownership calculation**: The set is a chronological image slice, not a certified run sheet. Fractional mineral deeds, estate shares, reservations, exceptions, survivorship, and later transfers cannot be safely netted without all predecessors and successors.

- Compare the completed record chain with current OCC well, spacing/pooling, operator, production, and completion data and with current division-order, pay-deck, JIB, and lease-maintenance records.
- Verify present pipeline location, use, abandonment, operator, and easement succession; do not treat utility mortgage releases as easement releases.
