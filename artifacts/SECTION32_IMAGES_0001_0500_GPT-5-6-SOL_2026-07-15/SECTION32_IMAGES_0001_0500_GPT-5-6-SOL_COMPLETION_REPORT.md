## Major Diversified findings

No instrument in images 0001–0500 names Diversified or directly proves that Diversified is a lessee, assignee, working-interest owner, royalty/ORRI owner, mineral owner, operator, lien party, or recorded successor.

The image set is nevertheless material historical-chain evidence. It shows:

- Section 32 leasehold acquisitions by The Carter Oil Company and later stated-percentage assignments to seven assignees.
- Historical royalty and ORRI reservations/transfers that may burden later leasehold.
- Numerous mineral severances across all four quarters.
- Pipeline easements in the NW/4 and NE/4; utility mortgage releases did not release the underlying easements.
- Historical fee, leasehold, royalty, and utility liens plus selected releases.

These findings may affect a later-proved Diversified chain, but the required recorded succession bridge is absent from this image scope.
