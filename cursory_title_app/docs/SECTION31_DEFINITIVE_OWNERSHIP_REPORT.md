## 3. LEASEHOLD OWNERSHIP

### 3.1 Base oil-and-gas leases (OGL 1–63) — all HBP
Sixty-three base leases recorded 2004–2006 (Bk ~1736–1833), the EOG/Arrowhead
leasing play, cover 100% of the section's minerals. Representative lessors include
Carrie Leeann & Nancy Susan Mitchell, Gena W. Moore, the Bain family, Jensen,
Fuchs, Devenas, the Kirk family (OGL 62 = Ella Pearl Kirk), and the Marchelle
Sorenson Daigle trust (OGL 24). **All are carried held-by-production** by the
Alexander 1-31 unit well.

> **Legal basis (verify):** In Oklahoma an oil-and-gas lease in its secondary term
> continues only so long as the habendum "thereafter" condition is met; production
> from a single well on a properly pooled/unitized tract can hold every lease
> committed to the unit. *Pack v. Santa Fe Minerals*, 1994 OK 23, 869 P.2d 323
> (habendum / temporary-cessation doctrine); pooling under 52 O.S. §87.1; cf.
> *J.A. Harper v. Hudson Gas & Oil Corp.* (production from a well within a pooled
> unit holds the committed lease — jurisdiction to confirm). **HBP is a legal
> conclusion reserved to the examiner and is contingent on OTC production data
> not yet pulled (§7).**

> on termination of the base lease and is subject to the rule against perpetuities

> **subordinate and springing** — they convey nothing while OGL 1–63 remain HBP.
