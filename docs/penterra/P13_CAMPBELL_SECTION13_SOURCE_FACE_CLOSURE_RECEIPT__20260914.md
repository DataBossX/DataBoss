### Federal findings raised

**F-1 — County-style recorded date on a BLM action (SRP row 197). RAISE.**
The SRP row carries `Rec Date = 8/4/2026`. A BLM Serial Register Page is a printout of
current case status; it is not recorded in any county. `8/4/2026` is identical to the
workbook header `Date Posted Thru: 8/4/2026`, i.e. it is the **pull/print date**, not a
recording date. This is precisely the "manufactured county-style recorded date for a BLM
action" failure mode. It should be re-seated into a pull-date/comment field or blanked,
subject to Section 15 parity. **Flagged, not changed.**

**F-2 — Serial number in a party column (SRP row 198). RAISE.**
Row 198 carries `WYW-047318` in the **Grantee** column. That is a case serial, not a party.
Column-semantics defect. **Flagged, not changed.**

**F-3 — Part 4 completeness. OPEN.**
Part 4 contains exactly **one** row (page 1, "Notation of Commitment to Unit Agreement and
Communitization Agreements", WYW-109544X). Against Part 1's 100 rows this is a strong
under-abstraction signal. The 2026-09-09 source census recorded **462 BLM historical pages
with zero extracted text on 462/462 scanned pages**, so Part 4 cannot be certified complete
from the text cache. **Requires page-image vision over the Part 4 source before freeze.**
