# Title Examination Rules

The software applies workflow rules, not legal judgment. Every material fact must point to the controlling source and locator. Indexes, runsheets, OCR, prior reports, model agreement, and filenames are leads unless verified against the instrument. Preserve competing candidates and conflicts; never force a balance, infer a missing owner, or silently bridge a chain gap.

Examine instrument identity, execution and recording dates, parties/capacities, legal description, interest language, reservations/exceptions, burdens, cross-references, probate/court authority, and later amendments/releases. Confirm tract continuity and effective chronology. Missing exhibits, schedules, legibility, or source scope are release issues.

The generated runsheet and chain are drafts requiring a qualified examiner. Jurisdiction-specific recording, probate, marketable-title, curative, and interpretation rules must be supplied and approved by counsel; they are not encoded comprehensively here.
