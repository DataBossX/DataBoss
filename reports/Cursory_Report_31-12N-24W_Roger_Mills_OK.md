## PART 7 — HELD-BY-PRODUCTION (HBP) ANALYSIS *(client decision pending)*

**The question:** Does the one inactive well hold Section 31‑12N‑24W (or any portion / any depth) by production?

**Cursory answer: UNDETERMINED.** An *inactive* well does **not**, by itself, hold a lease. Under a standard Oklahoma habendum clause a lease continues past primary term only so long as there is **production in paying quantities** or operations/savings that substitute for it. Resolution turns on:

| Factor | What to check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Is the well actually producing? | Last production date; any production within the cessation window | OTC gross production; OCC Form 1000/1073 |
| Lease savings clauses | **Shut-in royalty**, cessation-of-production, continuous-operations, dry-hole clauses in the controlling lease(s) | Recorded lease (OKCountyRecords) |
| Were shut-in royalties timely paid? | Shut-in payments / division-order records | Client / operator records |
| Unit / pooling | Is the section in a unit where production *elsewhere in the unit* holds it? | OCC spacing & pooling orders |
| Temporary abandonment | OCC-approved TA status and its time limits | OCC well file |

**Three outcomes to present to client:**
- **(A) HBP intact** — well/unit still in paying production or validly shut-in within savings clause → existing lease(s) hold; mineral acquisition is of *leased* (HBP) minerals.
- **(B) Lease terminated / expired** — production long ceased, no valid savings → minerals are *open*; this is a **leasing/acquisition opportunity** (consistent with "bring the minerals forward" now).
- **(C) Mixed / depth-severed** — held only as to a producing formation or unitized depths; deeper/other rights open.

**Recommendation:** Bring the minerals forward to current ownership now (Parts 3–4) so the client can move immediately under **whichever** outcome they choose. Hold the final HBP call until the client advises how they want to treat the well **and** the well file + controlling lease are reviewed.
