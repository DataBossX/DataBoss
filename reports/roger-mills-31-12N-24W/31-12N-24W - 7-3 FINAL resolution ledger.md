## Method note
Matches require the SAME party (given name + surname must both appear on the grantee side, or
an exact entity match) via a conveyance-type instrument (deed/assignment/decree/order/patent),
acquired BEFORE the flagged conveyance. This rejected false positives the loose matcher first
produced (Hamilton↔Hazel Ayers; Bain↔William Bain / Proof-of-Death; Koala↔El Paso Production).
Rawdata was cross-referenced in addition to the Runsheet — the 1974 Kirk deed, 1986 Clarence
Bain MD, and 2009 Newell deed live there, not on the Runsheet. Not a certified opinion; confirm
each cited instrument against its recorded image.
