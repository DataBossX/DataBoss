Executive Summary

The whole section is leased and held by production; ownership is a family-mineral mosaic under a 2026 Silver Oak top-lease sweep

Section 31 is a fully-leased, held-by-production Cherokee section. Every net mineral acre sits under one of 63 base oil-and-gas leases (the 2004–2006 EOG / Arrowhead play, OGL 1–63), all held by production by the active Alexander 1-31 unit well. In 2026 Silver Oak Natural Resources, LLC took a six-lease top-lease sweep (OGL 64–69, 3/16 royalty) over the principal family mineral interests — subordinate and springing until the base leases terminate. Fee mineral ownership is a mosaic dominated by the Mitchell / Williams / Moore, Bain, Kirk, and Daigle / Sorenson / Johnson families, with modern mineral-fund buyers layered on top.

What is firm. The section is 100% leased; base leases are HBP by the Alexander 1-31 unit (OCC status active, last reported production 3-1-2026); the Silver Oak top-lease package (OGL 64–69) is recorded and mapped to its lessors; the wellbore working-interest chain is traced to its current holder; four tracts (1, 3, 8, 9) are fully owner-reconciled and every tract grid foots to its gross acreage.

What remains open. Exact conveyed fractions on some instruments, formal HBP confirmation via OTC production, well-completion detail (Form 1002A), and a set of pre-2000 root-of-title source-in links require recorded instrument images not obtainable in the preparation environment. These are itemized in Open Items — none are fabricated; each names the document to pull.
