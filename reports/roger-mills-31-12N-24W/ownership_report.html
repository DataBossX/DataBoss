Executive Summary

Fully leased and production-carried; ownership is a family-mineral mosaic that is ~47% owner-identified and honestly open elsewhere

Section 31 is a held-by-production Cherokee section. Every net mineral acre is carried under one of 63 base oil-and-gas leases (the 2004–2006 EOG / Arrowhead play, OGL 1–63), held by the active Alexander 1-31 unit well, with a 2026 Silver Oak Natural Resources top-lease sweep (OGL 64–69, 3/16) over the principal family interests. Fee mineral ownership is dominated by the Mitchell / Williams / Moore, Bain / Jensen, Kirk, and Daigle / Sorenson / Johnson families, with modern mineral funds layered on. Of the 637.42 net acres, roughly 301.6 (≈47%) are owner-identified to a named holder; the balance is carried as HBP base-lease minerals and disclosed as open pending the recorded source images — not force-allocated.

Firm. 100% leased; base leases carried HBP by the active unit (last reported production 3-1-2026); Silver Oak top leases OGL 64–69 recorded and mapped to their lessors; the wellbore working-interest chain is traced to its current holder; Tracts 1, 3, 6, 8, 10 balance to gross; every tract grid foots to its acreage; the section ties to 637.42.
Open / requires curative. Tracts 2, 4, 5, 7, 9 carry large disclosed open balances; the Dold succession rests on an affidavit of heirship (not probate); the Ella Pearl Kirk estate (48.80 NMA) needs heirship; HBP and 640-ac spacing need OCC/OTC proof; 21 WI assignments lack stated percentages. All itemized in Requirements and Exceptions. Nothing is fabricated.

Exceptions / Title Issues

Standing exceptions a purchaser takes subject to

  • Over-conveyance / negative retained NMA on Tracts 3, 4, 5, 7, 9, 10 — numerous grid rows carry negative final NMA (owners conveyed more than they held of record); flagged for landman review, not force-balanced.
  • Potential over-lease — several base leases state NMA exceeding the lessor's normalized title (e.g., Nancy Susan Mitchell OGL 2 at 100.17; A. C. Campbell OGL 9; Clarence C. Bain OGL 11). A lease is not treated as proof of mineral ownership.
  • Metes-and-bounds / exception legals on Tracts 5, 6, 8, 9 — long exception-based descriptions; acreage drives every NMA and must be verified against the plat / GLO survey.
  • Ambiguous vesting — "(and/or)" and joint-vs-several language unresolved on several rows; resolve from instrument images before final allocation.
  • Unreleased liens — mortgage / lien burden chain open until each is matched to a release.
